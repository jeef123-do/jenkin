EVERYDAY WELLNESS

Health Tips

Practical ideas to help build habits that feel sustainable.

✳
There’s no one-size-fits-all routine.

Choose what feels realistic for you, and make changes at your own pace.

⌁

NOURISH

Build balanced meals

When it works for you, include a mix of vegetables or fruit, sources of protein, and satisfying grains or other carbohydrates. Variety matters more than perfection.

Make one meal a little more colorful.
↗

MOVE

Find movement you enjoy

Walking, dancing, stretching, gardening, or a favorite sport all count as ways to move. Start where you are and take breaks when you need them.

A short walk can be a lovely start.
☾

REST & HYDRATE

Support your daily rhythm

Regular sleep routines and drinking fluids throughout the day can support general wellbeing. Individual needs differ, so notice what helps you feel your best.

Try a calming wind-down routine.
✦

KEEP IT SUSTAINABLE

Make changes that stick

Small, repeatable habits are often easier to maintain than big overhauls. Setbacks are normal; simply return to a routine that feels manageable.

Pick one habit to practice this week.

Informational only: These general wellness ideas are educational, not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional about what is right for you.
